Diagnostics

RSSI is a relative radio-strength measurement, not a precise distance. Walls, your body, antenna orientation, reflections, and the device's transmit power can move the reading substantially. For best results: stand still for a few seconds at each position, then move in one direction and compare the smoothed reading.
Use only to locate devices you own or are authorized to locate. This tool does not obtain GPS location, cannot find a device outside Bluetooth radio range, and may not detect classic-Bluetooth-only or non-advertising devices.
