About the Instruments

These are fully synthesized acoustic approximations, not recordings. The new engine models each instrument differently: trumpet emphasizes a bright brass harmonic series and lip-like pitch behavior; saxophone adds reed saturation, breath noise and formants; tuba uses a darker low-frequency harmonic structure, slower attack and resonant body. Everything remains self-contained and offline.
